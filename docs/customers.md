# Customer Accounts

Customer accounts allow sales to be associated with a specific customer.

## Add a customer

Open:

**More → Customers → Add**

Available fields include:

- Customer barcode
- Name
- Phone
- Email
- Address
- Notes

If the barcode is left blank, the app generates a customer barcode automatically.

## Barcode lookup

Use the scanner on the Customers screen or POS customer selector.

- Known barcode → opens/selects the customer.
- Unknown barcode in customer registration → starts a new customer entry using that barcode.

## POS usage

On the POS screen, tap the Customer card to:

- select a registered customer
- search by name/barcode/phone
- scan a customer barcode
- switch back to Walk-in

A customer is required for **Account Payable** transactions.
