# Products

## Product list

Product cards show the product image, name, barcode/SKU, price, and stock information.

Tap a product card to edit it.

## Product images

Images can be selected from the device and stored locally by the app. Product images are displayed throughout the product, inventory, and POS interfaces.

## Barcodes

A product barcode can be entered manually or scanned.

The POS normalizes common scanner input and supports common UPC-A / EAN-13 leading-zero differences.

## Inventory-enabled vs non-inventory items

Inventory-enabled products maintain stock quantities and create inventory transactions when sold or adjusted.

Non-inventory items can still be sold but do not reduce stock.

## Suppliers

Products can be linked to one or multiple suppliers.
