# Quick Start

## 1. Add products

Open **Products** and add the items you sell.

Recommended fields:

- Name
- Barcode
- Selling price
- Cost
- Unit
- Opening stock
- Product image
- Supplier

You can also scan a product barcode while adding/searching.

## 2. Configure inventory

Open **Inventory** to review current stock and stock movements. Tap an item card to adjust stock.

## 3. Add customers when needed

Open **More → Customers**.

Customers can have:

- Name
- Customer barcode
- Phone
- Email
- Address
- Notes

The barcode can be generated automatically or assigned manually.

## 4. Make a sale

Open **POS**.

1. Scan or search for a product.
2. Enter quantity.
3. Add more items as needed.
4. Optionally select a customer.
5. Tap **Checkout**.
6. Choose the payment mode.
7. Save the sale.
8. Print the receipt if a printer is configured.

## 5. Review transactions

Open **Sales** to view transactions, totals, customer names, payments, and outstanding Account Payable balances.
